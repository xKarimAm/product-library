# Product Library live demo

A city of 20 projects: six hand-made heroes you click through, plus a generated crowd that makes
it a skyline. Every building is in a real state, so one screen tells the whole story: where every
project stands, which notes can still be trusted, and what to do next. The centrepiece is live: you
commit to a project on stage and watch Product Library stop trusting its notes.

The talk that goes with it (what I built, why, the tech, what builders can take away) is
`docs/presentation.html`. Open it in the same browser and switch tabs.

## Before you present

Within the hour before you go on (every timestamp is relative to "now"):

    py demo/make_demo.py --rehearse    # pre-flight, about 30 seconds
    py demo/make_demo.py --serve       # then open http://127.0.0.1:7777

`--rehearse` builds the demo, checks every product landed in its intended state, runs the live
moment end to end (PASS, live commit, STALE, checkpoint, PASS) and resets the demo. If it prints
`rehearsal OK`, the show will work. `--serve` rebuilds and starts Product Library on the 20 demo buildings only;
your real `~/.claude` projects are not shown and nothing there is read or written. Stop it with
Ctrl+C.

Run every command from the repo folder (`cd` into it first). Keep a **second terminal** open there for the live moment.

**Projector check:** the layout is verified at 1920x1080 and 1280x720. Moving or resizing the
window re-lays the city out at once. Press F11 for a true full screen.

**Motion check (do this on the presenting laptop):** Windows Settings > Accessibility > Visual
effects > **Animation effects ON**. With it off, the browser asks every page for reduced motion
and the city stands still: no traffic, no villagers, no blimp, no flickering windows. That is
correct behaviour, and the wrong look for a stage.

**Time of day:** the sky follows the real clock (night lights every window, searchlights sweep).
To force it, open `http://127.0.0.1:7777/?hour=22` for night or `?hour=12` for day.

## The city at a glance

How far along a project is reads as its building: **dirt lot** (registry entry, nothing on disk),
**tent** (just started: defining it, or no checkpoint yet), **low-rise** (finding its footing),
**tower** (lots of work). A crane means it is active with a checkpoint. The colour legend is at
the bottom of the right-hand rail.

## The six heroes

| Product | Stage | What it shows on screen |
|---|---|---|
| calculator | ship | **Gate PASS.** Proof is the real result of running its 6 unit tests, plus the commit hash. Clean git. The one you break live. |
| chatbot-frontend | build | **Waiting on you.** Clay roof and a "1" flag: the model provider decision is yours. Uncommitted work shows as "1 dirty". |
| habit-tracker | verify | **STALE.** A commit landed 5 hours after the last checkpoint, so its NEXT note may describe work already done. Dashed roof outline, STALE badge, gate FAIL on "fresh". |
| url-shortener | define | **Gate FAIL with soft gaps.** NEXT says only "continue", one blocker has no owner, no proof recorded. Red roof: blocked on someone else. |
| pomodoro-timer | ship | **Proof that cites a commit that doesn't exist.** Tests are real, but the cited commit `4f9e2a1` was never made. Product Library asks git; the gate still passes (soft gap) and asks about the commit. |
| weather-widget | (none) | **No state at all.** An idea from weeks ago, sitting dormant in the back row. |

Open **Full view** on any hero to show depth: a real commit history (calculator has 8 commits, habit-tracker
works on a `streak-edge-cases` branch), recent prompts, a dated HANDOFF.md session log, Claude's MEMORY.md and
CLAUDE.md. **Plans** in the header shows four demo plans; your real `~/.claude/plans` is never read.

The crowd (towers like kanban-board and invoice-parser, tents like qr-generator, two empty lots)
is generated by `EXTRA` and `LOTS` in `demo/make_demo.py`; every one is self-checked like the heroes.

## Talk track (about 8 minutes)

1. **The city (45s).** "Every building is a project I started with Claude. Tents just started,
   towers have months of work, dirt lots never got going. Front: touched in the last 30 days. Back:
   dormant. Colour is state: clay means a decision is waiting on me, red is blocked on someone
   else, violet has a written checkpoint." Point at the "waiting on you" pill and the legend.
2. **calculator: what good looks like (45s).** Click it. "Stage ship, gate PASS. The proof is not a
   claim: it is the test run and the commit." Read the PROOF line aloud.
3. **habit-tracker: the note that lies (45s).** Click it. "The NEXT note was written six hours ago,
   then someone committed. The note may now be wrong, so it is labelled 'written before the latest
   work' and the gate fails." Open **Full view** and show the STALE STATE line with the hours.
4. **Live: break the calculator (75s).** "Watch the good one." In the second terminal:

       py demo/make_demo.py --commit calculator

   Wait about ten seconds, press **F5**. The calculator now has the STALE pill, a dashed roof and
   gate FAIL. "I changed the code; the note didn't change; Product Library noticed." Then repair it the way a
   real session ends:

       py demo/make_demo.py --checkpoint calculator

   Press **F5**: back to PASS, and the PROOF line now cites the new commit.
   (Its age stays "2h ago": that counts Claude sessions, not commits.)
5. **url-shortener: weak evidence (45s).** Click it. "The gate does not just pass or fail, it asks
   the one question to answer next: 'continue' is not a next step." Mention the soft gaps: an
   unowned blocker and no proof.
6. **pomodoro-timer: proof is checked, not trusted (30s).** Click it. "It says shipped, tests
   pass, commit 4f9e2a1. Product Library asked git: that commit isn't in the repo." Read the gate question
   aloud. A model can quote evidence it never produced; this is the check that catches it.
7. **The catalog card: why does this exist? (45s).** Click chatbot-frontend and read FOR and
   WHY IT MATTERS. "Months from now I'll find this and know who it was for and why I cared. The
   reason quotes a tester word for word, and Product Library checked that the words are really in
   FEEDBACK.md." Then click invoice-parser, the tallest tower: "Its reason quotes accounts saying
   two hours a *day*. The notes say two hours a *week*. Caught." Then url-shortener: no card, so
   it asks "Would future-you know who this is for and why it mattered?"
8. **chatbot-frontend: decisions (30s).** Click it. "A blocker tagged with my name becomes 'waiting
   on you' in the header. That's the list I look at every morning."
9. **The report (45s).** Click **Report** in the header. "One table across every project: failing
   gates first, each with its next question. This is the portfolio view."
10. **Where the data comes from (30s).** "Each project carries one file, written in thirty seconds at
   the end of a session by the `/coc-checkpoint` skill. Product Library only ever reads."

## If something looks wrong

- **Ages look off ("1d ago" on everything):** the demo is old. Rebuild with `--serve`.
- **The page stopped updating after a rebuild:** it reloads itself on its next 30-second refresh;
  press F5 to do it now.
- **`--commit` didn't flip it:** wait the full ten seconds before F5.
- **`Only one usage of each socket address` / port 7777 in use:** another Product Library is still running.
  Stop it first, or in PowerShell use another port: `$env:COC_PORT=7788; py demo/make_demo.py --serve`.
- **`could not clear ... coc-demo`:** a demo server, terminal or Explorer window is still inside the demo
  folder. Close it and run the command again; nothing is lost.
- **`demo check FAILED` or a failed rehearsal:** the message names the product and the field that is
  wrong; Product Library or the scenario in `demo/make_demo.py` changed. Fix it before presenting.
